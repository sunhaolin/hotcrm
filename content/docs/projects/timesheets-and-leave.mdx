# Timesheets & Leave

A **timesheet** (工时表, `crm_timesheet`) is one person's hours on one project for one month, and the record that turns those hours into **labor cost**. A **leave request** (请假申请, `crm_leave_request`) is the absence that shortens the month, and once approved it writes itself into the timesheet as leave hours.

## What a timesheet record stores

| Field | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Submitter, Month | Whose hours, which month — one sheet per person, project and month |
| Delivery Project *or* Presales Project | Where the hours are booked; a sheet on a presales project rolls into that bid's Presales Hours |
| Grade / Rate Card | The [rate card](/docs/projects/master-data) row that prices the hours |
| Hourly Rate | Filled from the rate card |
| Standard Hours, Leave Hours, Overtime Hours, Hours | The month's arithmetic — see below |
| Cost | Hours × hourly rate |
| Approval Status *(read-only)*, Approved Date | The **工时审批** verdict |

## What the system does automatically

Four hooks run on every save, in this order:

1. **Rate fill** (`timesheet_rate_fill`) — picking a grade copies that rate card's hourly rate onto the sheet, so a rate change on the card reaches new sheets and leaves approved history alone.
2. **Attendance sync** (`timesheet_attendance_sync`) — on a *draft* sheet, **Hours = Standard Hours − Leave Hours + Overtime Hours**. Standard hours are what the month owes; leave and overtime move the figure. Once the sheet is submitted the hours stand.
3. **Cost fill** (`timesheet_cost_fill`) — **Cost = Hours × Hourly Rate**, recomputed on every edit of either.
4. **The budget gate** (`timesheet_budget_gate`) — a sheet the project's budget cannot pay for is **refused**, on the save that creates it. Two ways: the project's actual cost has already reached its current budget — *项目「…」成本已超预算（实际 … ≥ 预算 …），工时填报已限制* — or it still has budget left but less than this sheet costs — *项目「…」预算剩余 …，本次工时成本 … 已超出剩余预算，工时填报已限制；请调减工时，或先提交预算调整并审批通过*. The control line is the **current budget**: the baseline plus every approved [budget adjustment](/docs/projects/delivery-projects), so an approved adjustment is what lets people book to the project again. A project with no budget at all is not gated.

### Approval, and what counts
Click **Submit for Approval**; the sales manager decides; the status is read-only otherwise. **Only an approved timesheet is labor cost**: the delivery project's Labor Actual, the presales project's Presales Labor Actual and the Project Cost dashboard all sum approved sheets and nothing else. A draft sheet is a plan; a rejected sheet is nothing.

## Leave requests

A leave request carries a type (*annual, sick, personal, compensatory, marriage, maternity, other*), a start and end date, the days between them — computed by `leave_days_fill`, not typed — and optionally the delivery project the absence affects. It is approved by the **请假审批** flow (a department head).

### Leave into the timesheet
When a leave request is **approved**, `leave_timesheet_sync` finds the requester's timesheet for that month and writes the leave into its **Leave Hours**; the attendance sync above then lowers the month's hours and the cost follows. Leave that is still pending or was rejected touches no timesheet.

## Sharing

Timesheets are *Controlled by Parent*: whoever can see the delivery project sees its sheets, and nobody else — a rep who cannot open the project cannot open a sheet on it. Leave requests are **private**: the requester's own, plus the Sales Manager and System Administrator, who read all.

## Tips

- ✅ **One sheet per month, submitted at month end.** Hours the project's remaining budget cannot cover bounce on save; ask for the adjustment first.
- ✅ **File leave before the timesheet is submitted.** Leave approved afterwards does not reopen a submitted sheet.
- ✅ **Use the presales project for bid work**, so it shows up on the bid's Presales Labor Actual rather than on a delivery project.
