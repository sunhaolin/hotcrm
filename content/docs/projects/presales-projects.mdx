# Presales Projects

A **presales project** (售前项目, `crm_presales_project`) is the bid: the work a sales and solution team does between an initiated opportunity and a signed contract. It hangs off the opportunity and its account, carries the **Bizcase** — the cost estimate and the quote — and is what the executive approves before a delivery project can be opened.

## What a presales project record stores

| Group | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Project Information** | Name, Alias, Opportunity, Account, Project Type, Business Category, planned dates, Description |
| **Project Roles** | Account Manager, Project Manager, Project Director, Project QA, Pricing Manager — five user lookups, so the record names who owns each part of the bid |
| **Cost Estimate & Quote** | Labor Cost, Third-Party Service Cost, Procurement Cost, Project Expense, Total Cost, Quote Amount, Gross Margin %, Presales Hours, Presales Labor Actual |
| **Information Security** | Security Class and a note — the classification a bid has to declare before it leaves the building |
| **Approval** | Approval Status *(read-only)*, Approved Date |

The four cost figures are **rollups of the presales project's current [cost plan](/docs/projects/cost-plans)** — its Bizcase, phase *Bizcase* — and **Total Cost** is their sum; **Gross Margin %** is derived from it and the quote amount. None of the five is typed in: plan the Bizcase on the cost plan and the figures follow.

**Presales Hours** and **Presales Labor Actual** are rollups: the hours and cost of every *approved* [timesheet](/docs/projects/timesheets-and-leave) booked against this presales project. A draft or rejected timesheet counts for nothing, so the figure on the record is the figure finance would accept.

## What the system does automatically

### The Opportunity picker only offers initiated deals
A bid may only reference an opportunity the business has already initiated, so the **Opportunity** picker lists only records whose *Initiation Status* is **Initiated** — the verdict of the 商机立项审批 flow. A deal still in *Not Initiated*, *Submitted* or *Rejected* does not appear until it clears that gate. This is separate from the amount-tiered Large Deal Approval on the opportunity, which does not affect the picker.

### The Account follows the Opportunity
Leave **Account** empty and it is filled from the opportunity you picked (`presales_project_account_carry`) — the customer is a fact the deal already holds, not something to look up twice. Re-point the bid at a different opportunity and the account moves with it, unless you had chosen an account yourself, which is always kept.

## Approval

The bid is approved by the **售前立项审批** flow. Click **Submit for Approval** at the top of the record — the *Approval Status* field itself is read-only, on the form and on the API, so there is no other way to move it. Submitting locks the record; the approval goes to an executive; on the verdict the status and the approved date are stamped and the owner is notified. A rejected bid can be corrected and resubmitted.

Only an **approved** presales project can have its Bizcase imported into a delivery project — see *Importing the Bizcase* on [Delivery Projects](/docs/projects/delivery-projects).

## Sharing

Presales projects are **private** and no sharing rule widens them: the owner reads their own, the Sales Manager and the System Administrator read all. An account reaching a rep through a territory rule does not bring the account's presales projects with it — the *Presales Projects* related list on such an account shows the rep their own only.

## Tips

- ✅ **Plan every cost category on the Bizcase cost plan, even when one is zero.** The delivery project imports that plan as its version 1, and a category nobody planned is a category nobody budgeted.
- ✅ **Book presales hours to the presales project, not to the opportunity.** Only a timesheet against the project rolls into Presales Labor Actual.
- ✅ **Attach the Bizcase document.** Files are enabled on presales projects.
