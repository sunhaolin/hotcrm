Steps 27–31 of the project-lifecycle requirement (成本计划管理) are rebuilt
without the demo's simplifications. Maintainer instruction, verbatim:
「这块需求不要简化，如何设计？」 → 「按设计实现。」

**FROM** one `crm_cost_plan_line` object holding all four cost categories, a
hand-typed month per row, a unit price that came from the rate card only when a
row happened to name one, and no way to tell travel from other expenses.
**TO** a three-layer model:

- **`crm_cost_plan`** — one *version* of a project's plan, hanging off a presales
  project (phase *Bizcase*) or a delivery project. It carries the frozen baseline
  and rolls up every total from the month ledger; `is_current` marks the version
  the project reads. **Import Bizcase Budget** (`import_bizcase_budget`, the
  requirement's step 27) clones the approved Bizcase as delivery plan v1 and
  freezes the baseline; **New Plan Version** clones an approved plan into the
  next draft; a budget adjustment that names a version takes its amount from
  the version totals and, once approved, puts that version in force and marks
  the old one *Superseded* (`budget_adjustment_amount`,
  `budget_adjustment_version_flip`). Plans are approved by the finance lead
  through `cost_plan_approval`; a plan that left draft freezes its lines and
  months (`cost_plan_lock`, `RECORD_LOCKED`).
- **Four line objects**, one per category, each with its own inputs and its own
  price source: `crm_labor_cost_line` (grade × headcount × hours per month; the
  rate is copied from the rate card and re-resolved per month by grade, rate
  standard and effective interval), `crm_service_cost_line` (per person-month /
  per person-day / lump sum × supplier quote), `crm_procurement_cost_line`
  (category × quantity × unit price, landed or amortised) and
  `crm_expense_cost_line` (travel computed from a `crm_travel_standard`, every
  reimbursement type budgeted directly; the type rides down to the ledger).
- **`crm_cost_plan_month`** — the one ledger every total reads: one row per
  line per month, generated by `cost_line_decompose`, normalised and keyed by
  `cost_plan_month_fill` (`allocation_key` is field-level unique, i.e.
  tenant-scoped), with a *Manually Adjusted* flag that survives regeneration
  and a **Re-split** action per line that clears it.

The presales project's four Bizcase figures and the delivery project's
Planned Total are now rollups of the current plan version (a summary of a
summary; the engine recomputes a parent through a real update, so the cascade
holds). `crm_rate_card` gains `rate_standard`; `crm_budget_adjustment` gains
`crm_cost_plan`. Seeds ship a Bizcase per presales project and a delivery plan
v1 per delivery project as lines only — the ledger is generated on replay —
plus four travel standards; every demo baseline is now its Bizcase total.

Docs: a new *Cost Plans* page in three locales, and the delivery, presales,
master-data, module-index and sharing pages re-pointed. Guards updated for the
new roster (docs ledger, ownership exemptions, attachment roster, nav
stranding rule, refusal-site count), plus `test/hooks-runtime-cost-plan.test.ts`
pinning the decomposition, per-month rate resolution, lock, ledger keys and
adjustment amount.
